Sunlab Literature Library is a personal research library built around the public work of Lei Sun’s group at Westlake University. It is not an official university website.

Three collections, one set of research directions.

Sunlab Papers contains 28 group-publication records (including one accepted manuscript) and 8 preprints from the lab publication list ↗.

Earlier Work separately contains 37 records from Lei Sun’s earlier research, as classified in the source bibliography. These are not counted as current-group outputs. Counts refer to bibliographic records; a preprint and a journal article may represent versions of the same work.

Sharing Papers contains all 1,086 unique DOIs extracted from 168 weekly literature reports spanning 2022–2026. The source index includes both shared papers and cited references; inclusion does not verify that every paper was read in full. No full-text PDFs or private report content are hosted here.

How categories work

The five research directions are derived from the lab’s publication topics, with Other for work outside those directions or without a clear match. They are a navigation aid, not an official lab taxonomy.

Lab records were assigned using their titles and research context. Shared records receive one primary category using explicit title terms; matching lab DOIs inherit the lab category. These initial assignments are provisional. You can change a paper’s category in its details; your changes are saved in this browser only.

Metadata & reading notes

Shared titles and bibliographic metadata were checked against Crossref (1,079 records) and arXiv (7 records). Different DOIs remain separate, including preprint and journal versions. Ten example reading notes are retained in English and link to their stated reading basis; notes are not a substitute for reading the original paper.

Publication dates

Dates use public publication metadata from Crossref, publisher pages or the original preprint source. Online publication and journal issue dates may differ. Where a source only specifies a year or month, that precision is retained; missing dates are never inferred from report-sharing dates. Preprints show their initial release date where available.

Your reading list

Saved papers and category changes are stored only in this browser. Export DOI lists to keep a portable copy. This is an imported archive, not a live daily feed.
